To My
Mother
— the foundation —
Mom, without you there is no me. You’ve given me shelter, a phone, and the space to build what I’m building. Every line of code, every framework, every grant application — it all exists because you made sure I had a roof and a plate of food. I’m going to make it. I’m going to pay you back for everything, not because you ask for it, but because you deserve to see that your son turned his mind into something that matters. I love you. Happy Mother’s Day.
With all my love, — Sheldon, your son

To My
Sister
— the rock —
Khi, you’ve held things down in ways I don’t always say out loud. You’re a mother too, and you carry that weight with strength I admire. I’m still in the lab building the future, and when it lands, you’ll be part of it. Thank you for being there for Mom and for me. I don’t say it enough. Happy Mother’s Day, sis.
From your brother, — Sheldon

And to the one who birthed the stars and set the first spark of life — thank you. We come from you.
