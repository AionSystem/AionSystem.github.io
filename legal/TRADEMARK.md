## 3. Permitted Use

You may use the word marks **nominatively** to refer to AionSystem products or services, provided:

- The use is accurate and not misleading
- You do not imply endorsement or sponsorship by AionSystem
- You do not use the marks in a way that dilutes their distinctiveness

**Example (acceptable):**  
*“This pipeline is inspired by AionSystem’s Vibe‑to‑Container method.”*

**Example (not acceptable):**  
*“AionSystem‑certified” or “AionSystem approved” without written permission.*
