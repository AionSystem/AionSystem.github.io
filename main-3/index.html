The Method

Document to findings register in four phases. Typical engagement: 5–10 business days.

Phase 01 / 04
Adversarial Read
Document received: ISA Deep-Sea Mining Code, ISBA/29/C/WP.1

Reading posture: adversarial.
Question applied to every clause:
"How does this fail? Who benefits from the failure?"

→ Not editing. Not proofreading. Reading as the opposing party.
→ Every assumption surfaced. Every undefined term flagged.
Phase 02 / 04
Failure Node Mapping
Findings registered:
NODE-001 · Section 1.3 · CRITICAL
"Environmental protection obligation"
— Obligation stated without enforcement mechanism.
— No definition of "serious harm" in operative text.
— Annex II reference creates circular dependency.

→ CRITICAL: Obligation legally unenforceable from day one.

Severity Scoring

Cross-reference errors
