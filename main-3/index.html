How to Work With Me

Read BeforeYou Send

These rules exist so your document gets my full attention. If you can't follow them, don't send anything. Minimum engagement $1,200. No discounts. No exceptions.

// 00

Identity & Boundaries

Who I Am

I find what kills a document before it costs you. Red-Team Framework Designer. Structural Integrity Analyst. Modern Advocatus Diaboli. I read documents. I find the seams. I report them.

AI Usage

AI runs the instruments. I decide what's load-bearing. The judgment is human. Always.

Legal Disclaimer

This is structural analysis, not legal advice. I find and name structural failure nodes. I do not certify a document as legally sound, safe, or compliant.

Liability

My total liability is limited to the fee paid. I am not your insurance, your lawyer, or your compliance officer.

// 01

Who I Work With & Refuse

Global Neutrality

Any country. Any company. Anywhere. I take no sides. Your politics are not my politics. The document is the only thing in the room.

Refusals

Pushy? Threats? Pressure? Claims to improve my business? Talk down to me? I refuse. Also: I will not red-team another company's document for you to become a bad actor. Morals are subjective. Mine are mine.

War & Military

I do not deal with war documents or military strategy. Not because I can't. Because I know what I'm capable of building. Some doors stay closed.

// 02

Engagement & Payment

How Engagement Works

1. You send the document. 2. I confirm scope and price. 3. You pay before work begins. 4. I run the method. 5. I send the written report. That's it.

Payment

Stripe payment link. You click. You pay. The money clears. I start. No invoices. No net-30. No crypto. No wire transfers. No checks. Payment first. Then work.

Minimum Engagement & No Discounts

Every engagement starts at $1,200. No discounts, no exceptions — not for nonprofits, startups, volume, referrals, or anyone else. The price is the price. Asking for a discount ends the conversation.

Refunds & Chargebacks

No refunds once work has started. Chargeback? The engagement is void. Future work declined.

Engagement Models

Pricing is fixed per service tier. Custom scopes are quoted individually. See the Services page for current tiers and prices.

// 03

Communication Rules

Email Reading Days

I read emails on Tuesdays and Thursdays only. Anything else waits or gets ignored. I prefer people who pay attention.

Email Reply Days

I reply on weekends. Because by then I've had days to think and test angles.

Scheduled Outbox

If I finish early, it sits until the weekend. If I think of a new angle, I pull it back and rework before it reaches you.

Phone Calls

I prefer email or forms. Calls are scheduled, paid, and triple my standard rate. Unscheduled calls are ignored.

Forms vs. Structured Email

Use the website forms. Or structured email. Tell me what you need, what the document is, what worries you, what outcome you want.

// 04

Delivery & Scope

Standard Delivery

5–10 business days. This is the pace I recommend. It gives me time to think creatively.

Rushed Delivery

You pay more. Rushed work isn't the same work done quicker. It's a different tier of analysis. I add more frameworks because I have less time to sit and think.

Document Formats

PDF, .docx, .txt, or a readable link. Length and overage fees confirmed before payment.

No Guarantee of Finding

A clean pass means I didn't find a problem this pass. It doesn't mean the document is perfect. My job is to think more creatively than a generative AI bad actor would.

// 05

Confidentiality & Ownership

Confidentiality

Every document is confidential by default. NDA or no NDA. Not shared, posted, or discussed unless authorized.

Ownership

The report is yours once paid. I retain the method. Case study rights only with written permission, unless the document was already public.

Data Protection

Stored securely during review. Deleted on request after engagement closes. No training on your document. No retention beyond what's needed.

// 06

Disagreements & Capacity

Disagreements

Send a written rebuttal. I review once. If it changes my analysis, I revise free. If not, the finding stands. We don't go back and forth.

Capacity

I work alone. When the queue is full, I say so. First paid engagement gets the slot.

Exclusivity & Silence

Public document you don't want as a case study? Commission exclusivity before I start. Same confidentiality. No report. No case study. No DOI.
