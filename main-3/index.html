What is Red‑Teaming?

Red‑teaming is adversarial review — reading your document the way an opponent would, before they do. Traditional proofreading fixes grammar. I look for the structural failure modes: undefined terms, missing enforcement mechanisms, circular dependencies, and obligations without triggers. Then I provide resolution text that closes each gap while preserving your intent.

It’s not about being paranoid. It’s about being prepared.

What I Do

Adversarial Reading

Every document is read as the opposing party before it’s read as anything else. The author’s intent is secondary to the document’s actual operative effect.

Structural Findings

Undefined terms, missing enforcement mechanisms, circular dependencies, obligations without triggers. The places where the document’s own logic breaks.

Resolution Text

Every finding ships with ready-to-insert language that closes the gap while preserving the drafter’s intent. Not a problem list — a corrected document.

// 02

The Method

Foundry

Grounding. Every claim tied to the document’s own text. No invented mechanics.

Anvil

First attack. PDE, PROPORTION, EPE, TDAF. Every clause tested.

Crucible

Independent second attack. New frameworks, new lens. No conceptual echo.

Temper

Negation testing. Weak findings killed. Strong findings survive.

Resolution

Fix architecture. Immunization Node named. The failure can’t reassert.

// 03

Why This Matters

Most document failure isn’t caused by careless authors. It’s caused by authors reading their own work as supporters, not adversaries. The clause that seems clear to the drafter is exploitable by the party on the other side of the table.

I read every document as the opposing party — not to find rhetorical weaknesses, but to find the structural failure modes: undefined thresholds, missing enforcement mechanisms, circular dependencies, obligations stated without triggering conditions.
