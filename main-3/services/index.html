Closing every angle in a document at the time of review does not mean the document stays closed. AI capability advances, new exploitation techniques surface, and the document itself gets amended after delivery — each of which can reopen a gap that was correctly closed at review time.

A baseline review (Targeted Pass, Full Structural Review, or Comprehensive Adversarial Review) is required first. Every cycle after that is a delta scan against the established baseline: what changed, and what new exploitation angles emerged since the last cycle.

No permanent-safety claim is made. Instead, a Priority Remediation Rate applies to any prior scope — 50% off a Targeted Pass — if a Critical finding surfaces later.
