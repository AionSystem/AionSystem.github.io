◈ What a Finding Looks Like

Document: A vendor contract with a liability cap. The cap is defined. The exceptions are listed. The definition of "gross negligence" is left to applicable law.

The seam: between the liability cap and the undefined "gross negligence." Squeeze: vendor claims ordinary performance; customer claims gross negligence. The text provides no test.

Escape path: vendor breaches → customer sues → vendor invokes cap → customer argues gross negligence → a court must define a term the contract left open. Outcome: the litigation and uncertainty the author intended to avoid.

Severity: S3 — structural, exploitable with consequence. This is the level of specificity every pass delivers.

Before You Send a Document

What do I actually receive?

A written report of findings in your document. Every finding is severity-rated, tied to the exact clause or section, and — from the Anvil Pass up — paired with ready-to-insert resolution language. You are buying findings and fixes, not a certificate of attendance.

How do I know this will find something?

You don't — and that's exactly why the floor is a mutual test. The Scout Pass exists to prove the method on your document before you commit further, and the three published reviews above are the method already running on real, high-stakes documents, unsolicited and uncompensated. You're not paying on faith; you're paying for a demonstrated instrument pointed at your document.

What if I need something between tiers?

Tell me the document and the stakes at intake. I'll point you at the tier that fits the page count and the consequence — or scope a custom build (The AION Build) if none of the fixed tiers do.
