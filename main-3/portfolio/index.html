What This Is

These are adversarial reviews of major public documents. Each paper shows the full red-team process: dozens of attack passes, independent role councils, multi-model stress testing, and a public verdict.

If your organization relies on a document that has never been attacked this way — a contract, a policy, a regulatory filing, an AI governance framework — then it has undiscovered structural weaknesses. The proof below demonstrates what happens when I find them.

This is not academic writing. This is applied structural failure analysis.

Paper 01 · Dec 2025
The Hippocratic Oath
22 Passes 15-Role Council Multi-Model Gauntlet Verdict

Key Finding

"Do no harm" has no definition, no enforcement mechanism, and no adjudicating body. The most trusted promise in medicine is structurally hollow — and AI governance frameworks are now being tethered to it.

A full adversarial structural review of the Hippocratic Oath and the Declaration of Geneva, with a hardened 2026 reconstruction. Six adversarial review passes, three internal 15-role council rounds, a combined SAR → LEGAL → ANTI-FORGE run, a reconciliation pass, and a currency/delta pass. The review demonstrates that both documents are non-binding, unenforceable, and update-less assertions of intent.

The paper introduces a six-category harm taxonomy, an independent counter-expert requirement, technological update triggers, and distinct obligation tracks for human physicians and AI systems. Every finding is tied to evidence. Every limitation is named.
