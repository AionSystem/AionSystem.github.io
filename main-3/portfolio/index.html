Hippocratic Oath & Declaration of Geneva
Foundational Medical Ethics · Adversarial Review · Hardened 2026 Reconstruction
21 findings 6-category taxonomy 7 clauses Published DOI

Medicine's most trusted promise has no enforcement mechanism, no functional definition of "harm," no falsifiable standard, and no technological update trigger. This review attacks both the Hippocratic Oath and the Declaration of Geneva line by line, then reconstructs the Oath as a binding covenant with defined terms, external adjudication, and AI-surgical oversight.

Core finding: "Do no harm" is a rhetorical norm, not a verifiable commitment. The documents are aspirational declarations, not enforceable contracts. The AI era is about to inherit this structural void at scale.

The Hardened 2026 Reconstruction introduces a six-category harm taxonomy calibrated to physician agency, an independent counter-expert review requirement, explicit technological update triggers (90-day comprehensive adversarial testing), and distinct obligation tracks for human physicians and AI systems.

Anchored to existing frameworks — EU AI Act (Annex III), EU MDR, GDPR/HIPAA — not invented abstractions. Every limitation named, including authorship disputes and source verification constraints.

Published with DOI. Full adversarial review and hardened reconstruction available for download.
